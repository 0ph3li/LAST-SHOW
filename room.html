LAST SHOWthe roomrua da rosa 113

Hall

built in 1931 as a neighbourhood picture house, closed in 1987, opened again in 2019 by people who couldn’t stand walking past it in the dark. the tiles in the foyer are original. so is the draught.

since 1931

Screen

one screen, nine metres wide, a little warped on the left since the winter of 1996. every film is shown in the shape it was shot in, and the black curtains move to meet it: wide for la la land, almost square for in the mood for love.

Booth

a 35mm projector from 1962 and a digital one for the films that only exist as files. the projectionist changes reels by hand and has missed a changeover exactly once. ask her about it after the film.
